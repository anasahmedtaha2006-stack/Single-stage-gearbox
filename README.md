# Single-Stage Gearbox

A single-stage spur gearbox, fully modeled, drawn, and manufactured as a 3D-printed working assembly — from CAD to physical housing.

## Overview
This project covers the complete design cycle of a single-stage gear reduction unit: gear tooth calculations, shaft design, housing/cover design, oil seal integration, and full engineering drawings, followed by 3D-printed manufacturing of a working prototype.

## Gear Specifications
- **Module (m):** 2 mm
- **Teeth count (Z):** 20 teeth
- **Pitch diameter:** ⌀40 mm
- **Outer diameter:** ⌀60 mm
- **Bore diameter:** ⌀20 mm
- Shaft stepped down through ⌀25 / ⌀18 / ⌀15 mm sections to seat bearings and seals
- Oil seal integrated at the shaft exit for lubrication retention

## Housing & Cover Design
- Two-bearing-bore housing (⌀32 / ⌀40 mm bores) holding the input and output shafts at fixed center distance
- Cover plate secured with 6× M6×1 and 4× M5×0.8 bolt patterns
- Mounting holes (2× ⌀5, 2× ⌀7) for base fixing
- Full section views (C-C, D-D) drawn at 1:1 to verify internal clearances and bearing seats before manufacturing

## Manufacturing
The final housing was 3D printed (dual-color, blue/white shells) with:
- Bolted access covers over both shaft bores
- A keyed/D-shaft output stub for coupling to a load or motor
- Compact, sealed enclosure to protect the internal gear mesh

## Tech Stack
`SolidWorks` `GD&T` `Gear Design` `3D Printing` `Mechanical Assembly`
